Administrative texts are full of formulas: fixed openings, decision phrases and closing formulae. They signal which
part of a document template a text block fills, much as printed fields do on later forms. `formulation` finds and
types these templated openings, decisions and related skeletons in the REPUBLIC resolutions. It builds on the
`formula_detection` package and extends it to variants with different word order or word choice, and to formula
boundaries.
