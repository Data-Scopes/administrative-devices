Archives are hierarchical, and the hierarchy carries meaning. The titles of series, files and items name document
genres, procedures, actors, places, topics and periods. Language of the Archive (LotA) uses these titles from
Encoded Archival Descriptions as labels for the transcribed text below them, and asks:

- How does language differ across the parts of an archive?
- Which words and phrases belong to a genre or procedure, and which to a topic, place or actor?
- Can the archive's own structure help categorise its content at the level of files and pages?
