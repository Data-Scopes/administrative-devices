A new segmentation of the resolutions of the Dutch States General in the
[REPUBLIC](https://republic.huygens.knaw.nl/) corpus (Nationaal Archief 1.01.02 and 1.10.94). Each page is divided into
**sessions → resolutions → paragraphs**. Marginalia, attendance lists and page furniture (date headers, page numbers)
are typed separately. The project refactors and improves the upstream REPUBLIC segmentation, and every change is
measured against hand-made gold sets.
