A pipeline for named-entity matching and sequence alignment in the resolutions of the Dutch Republic (1626–1630 and
later). Entity enrichment itself is out of scope for Administrative Devices. This work is included because aligned
entities help to identify resolution structure and to link resolutions to one another.
