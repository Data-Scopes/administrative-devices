Segments the letter books (*brievenboeken*, registers of incoming letters) of the States General, NL-HaNA 1.01.02,
into letters, and links each letter to the resolutions that mention it.

- **Letter segmentation.** Find where each letter starts and ends (scan, region and line) and find its parts: the
  side heading with sender and date of receipt, the salutation, body, signature and enclosures.
- **Linking.** Connect letters to the resolutions that discuss them. This is the cross-reference layer that lets
  researchers trace a decision from the incoming correspondence to the final resolution.
